Platform Flywheel

Plus la plateforme est utilisée, plus l'école devient structurée, visible et efficace. Chaque étape alimente la suivante.

Supply Side

  • • Teachers publish courses
  • • Catalog becomes richer
  • • Courses live in Classroom

Demand Side

  • • Students discover courses
  • • Enroll with one click
  • • Access Classroom directly

Network Effect

  • • More courses = more students
  • • More students = more teachers
  • • Flywheel accelerates over time
