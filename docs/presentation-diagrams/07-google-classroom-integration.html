Google Classroom Integration

Metableton Ecole orchestre l'inscription et l'organisation. Google Classroom héberge l'espace pédagogique. Chacun fait ce qu'il fait le mieux.

What Metableton Does

  • • Course catalog & discovery
  • • Enrollment workflow
  • • Role management (RBAC)
  • • Dashboards per role

What Classroom Does

  • • Course content hosting
  • • Assignments & grading
  • • Student-teacher communication
  • • Google ecosystem (Drive, Meet, Calendar)

Key Insight

  • • Metableton does NOT replace Classroom
  • • It adds the missing management layer
  • • Each tool focuses on its strength
  • • No custom LMS to build or maintain
